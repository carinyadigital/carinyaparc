title: "Herbed Omelette with Native Greens"
date: "2025-06-22"
author: "jonno"
servings: 2
prepTime: "PT10M"
cookTime: "PT5M"
totalTime: "PT15M"
excerpt: "A vibrant, omega-rich start to your day using our pasture-raised eggs and foraged native greens."
tags: ["breakfast", "eggs", "greens"]
ingredients:
  - item: "4 pasture-raised Isa Brown eggs"
  - item: "1 cup assorted native greens (warrigal greens, river mint, saltbush)"
  - item: "1 tbsp butter or oil"
  - item: "Salt and pepper to taste"
instructions:
  - step: "Prep the greens: Wash and loosely chop native greens; pat dry."
  - step: "Whisk eggs: In a bowl, beat eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper."
  - step: "Cook: Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add greens and sauté for 1–2 minutes until just wilted. Pour in eggs."
  - step: "Finish: Lift edges as eggs set, tilting pan to cook evenly. Fold omelette in half and slide onto a plate. Enjoy immediately with a scattering of fresh herbs."
